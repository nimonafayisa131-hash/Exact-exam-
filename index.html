    This will delete all student results and attempt records. This cannot be undone!

    Delete All Authorized Students?

    This will delete the entire list of authorized students. After that, no student will be able to take the exam until they are added again.

    This will delete every teacher account. After that, none of them will be able to log in to view results until they are added again.
